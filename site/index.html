A PDF editor that stays out of the way.

Read

Sharp pages, fast.

Thumbnails, the outline and zoom down the side. Find any word across every page. Dark mode for the app, and dark pages for reading at night.

Mark up

Highlight, draw, box and type.

Select text and highlight it, draw freehand, add boxes and text in any colour. Move or delete a mark, with undo and redo.

Notes

Comments every app can read.

Notes are saved as real PDF comments, so they show up in Preview, Acrobat and the rest.

Sign

Draw it once.

Draw your signature and place it on any page. It is kept on your device for next time.

Forms

Fill in and tick.

Type into a PDF’s form fields and tick its boxes. What you enter is saved into the PDF.

Pages

Put pages in order.

Rotate, move, duplicate, insert a blank page or delete one. Drag thumbnails to reorder.

Merge and extract

Combine PDFs, or pull pages out.

Add other PDFs at the end, or save a few pages as a new PDF.

Passwords

Protected PDFs too.

Open a password-protected PDF, then choose whether the saved copy keeps the password. Passwords are never stored.

Export

Images, text, print and share.

Save a page as an image, the text as a .txt file, print, or share the PDF by Mail, Messages or AirDrop.

Your files

Save back to the file.

On the Mac, open a PDF from Finder and Save writes back to it. If another app changed it first, Cowdrey.PDF asks.

Documents

Everything you opened, kept.

PDFs you open, and your mark-up, are kept on this device, so you can pick up where you left off.

Private

Nothing leaves your device.

No account and no upload. Cowdrey.PDF works on the PDF right where it is, even offline in the Mac app.

Get Cowdrey.PDF.

Web

Any modern browser · Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox

Open Cowdrey.PDF
  1. Open it and drop a PDF in, or choose Open.
  2. Your PDFs and mark-up are kept in this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. Save PDF downloads the edited PDF. In Chrome and Edge it can save straight back to the file.

Mac

macOS 10.15 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Download for Mac
  1. Open Cowdrey.PDF.dmg and drag Cowdrey.PDF into Applications.
  2. This early version isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, Control-click Cowdrey.PDF and choose Open.
  3. To open every PDF with it: select one in Finder, press ⌘I, choose Cowdrey.PDF under Open with, then Change All.
